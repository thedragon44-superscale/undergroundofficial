Welcome to the underground. Street Code 101 is built on absolute privacy, sovereign finance, and ruthless reputation. Here is how to navigate your node:

1. Street Cred & The Ranking System

Your reputation is your currency. Every profile features a public +1/-1 voting system. You start as Alright 😐. Once you receive 5 total votes, your approval percentage dictates your true rank:

  • Square Business 🤝 (90-100%): Elite and universally trusted.
  • A-1 💯 (75-89%): Highly respected operators.
  • Alright 😐 (50-74%): Unproven, controversial, or fresh accounts.
  • Shady 🐍 (25-49%): Untrustworthy. Proceed with caution.
  • Rat 🐀 (0-24%): Burned by the network. Do not engage.

2. The Public Feed vs. Private Intel

Everything posted on the Global Feed (📡) is public to the entire network. Use it to build your Street Cred. For sensitive intel, strictly use Private Messages or Gang Chats.

3. True End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)

Your private communications are encrypted before they leave your device using a master key locked by your Vault Passphrase. The central servers literally cannot read your messages.

4. The Cryptographic Reality (CRITICAL)

Because the server cannot read your data, it cannot recover it. The only failsafe for a lost password is your 16-character Recovery Key. You must write this key down physically. If you lose your passphrase and your physical recovery key, your historical data is gone forever.

5. Financial Vault & Smart Escrow
