Terms of Service

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Cryptographic Architecture

Street Code 101 operates on a client-side, Zero-Knowledge End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) protocol. Private Direct Messages (DMs) and Gang Chat transmissions are encrypted on the user's local device prior to network routing.

  • No Server Inspection: The Platform operator does not possess, store, or maintain central private keys.
  • User Liability: Users assume 100% legal liability for all text, files, images, or code transmitted via encrypted channels.

2. Key Stewardship

Users are solely responsible for maintaining their Vault Passphrase and physical 16-character Recovery Key. Lost passphrases cannot be reset by administration.

3. StreetCoins & Treasury

StreetCoins are internal platform utility tokens. Fiat deposits via third-party gateways (Stripe) are subject to a non-refundable network processing fee of 6% + $0.30 USD.

4. Black Market & Smart Escrow

The Platform acts solely as a neutral software venue and escrow holder. Escrow disbursals algorithmically deduct a 5% platform treasury fee.

5. Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Public areas (Global Feed, User Profiles, Market Listings) strictly prohibit CSAM, physical violence threats, or malicious software distribution.

Network Manual

1. Interface Navigation

Welcome to the node. You have access to four primary sectors:

  • Network Feed: The global intelligence broadcast channel.
  • Black Market: The peer-to-peer commerce center.
  • E2EE Comms: Your private encrypted direct messages and group workspaces.
  • Capital Vault: The financial ledger for tracking your StreetCoins.

2. Secure Communications

All direct messages and workspaces utilize Zero-Knowledge E2EE. If you lose your passphrase and recovery key, your historical chat data is permanently unrecoverable.

3. The Sovereign Economy & Escrow

StreetCoins power the network. Use the Capital Vault to process fiat deposits (via Stripe). When trading on the Black Market, never send coins directly. Always use the Smart Escrow system.

4. The Street Cred System

Your reputation is permanently attached to your Operator Handle.

  • Square Business 🤝: Completing clean escrow deals and posting high-value intel earns positive votes.
  • Rat Status 🐀: Scammers and operators who fail to honor escrow will receive negative votes and be flagged.
